It dawned on me recently that I am extra ordinary in many regards. Of course I am a beautiful unique snowflake in spirit, but evidence is mounting indicating that I am actually pretty damn average in reality. Consider the following areas where I am doing an exceptional job of being "normal."

---

My given name at birth was Michael Steven Smith.

In the year I was born Michael was the number one most common US male baby name. It was actually the most common for several decades in a row.

My middle name Steven was number 27 on the list.

My last name Smith was and still is the most common US surname. 880 people in every 100,000 in the US have the last name Smith. It is also the most common surname of our neighbors up north in Canada.

Bonus fact: My biological father and stepfather are both also named Michael.

https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/decades/names1970s.html

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_common_surnames_in_North_America

My biological father and mother divorced.

According to the APA 40-50% of all US marriages suffer the same fate. My name changed when my stepfather adopted me.
