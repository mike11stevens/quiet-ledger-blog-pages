I repeatedly find myself reminding... myself... that everyone has 24 hours in a day. Everyone. Every one. Please feel free to slap me if I say "I don't have time"... because I DO. WE ALL DO.

![Austin Powers saying "Allow myself to introduce myself"]({{ '/assets/images/recovered/twenty-four-hours-allow-myself.png' | relative_url }})

What I don't have is time to make things a priority that aren't. To do this thing called life right, I think we need to make sure our choices on how to spend time align with our priorities. By making an effort to strike "I don't have time" from my speech, my hope is that the effort will help remind me to refocus on my priorities and bring them back to the forefront.

## More useful rabbit holes

- [Wait But Why: 100 Blocks a Day](https://waitbutwhy.com/2016/10/100-blocks-day.html) - Tim Urban turns a day into little blocks, which is exactly the kind of helpful/terrifying visual aid that makes you want to reorganize your life and then immediately take a snack break.
- [xkcd: 28-Hour Day](https://xkcd.com/320/) - the original comic for anyone considering the bold sleep strategy of "what if clocks were more of a suggestion?"
- [Four Thousand Weeks](https://www.oliverburkeman.com/books) - Oliver Burkeman's book-length reminder that time management is less about becoming a productivity wizard and more about accepting that the inbox will outlive us all. Comforting? Rude? Both.
